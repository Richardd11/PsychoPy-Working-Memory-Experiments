📊 EXPERIMENT DETAILS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

EXPERIMENT 1: SIMULTANEOUS
  • All 4 colored squares shown AT THE SAME TIME
  • 144 trials + 6 practice
  • ~40-50 minutes duration
  • File: experiment1_simultaneous.py

EXPERIMENT 2: SEQUENTIAL
  • 4 colored squares shown ONE AT A TIME
  • Parity tasks BETWEEN each square
  • 144 trials + 6 practice
  • ~45-55 minutes duration
  • File: experiment2_sequential.py

BOTH INCLUDE:
  • Cognitive load: 0, 2, or 4 digits
  • Memory test: Same/Different?
  • Thought probe: 8 categories
  • Complete data logging
